Controls & calibration

W A S D or arrows move. Hold Space to attack. 1 2 3 use skills with the game's auto-aim. C runs Skill 1 / Skill 2 / attack / ultimate / Skill 2 / attack. For aimed skills, drag the skill button on the live screen. Clicking the live screen sends a touch to your phone.

To recalibrate, enter a battle at full HP. Set each button center, select the two corners of the full green health bar, then save the battle HUD. Calibration clicks do not control the phone.
